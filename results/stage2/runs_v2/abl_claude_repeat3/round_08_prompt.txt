HOW EACH FACTOR IS TESTED
For each year 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025: train on Q1-Q2, score on Q3 (validation). Q4 is never used in this search.
The score is ROC AUC; "gain" is the change in AUC when a factor is added, averaged over Logistic Regression (LR)
and Random Forest (RF), and shown for each year and each model.
1. Alone: the factor is added to the 22 existing factors.
2. Team trial: the factor is added to the 22 factors plus the selected team.
3. Pair trial: promising factors of different roles are added two at a time.
A factor joins the SELECTED TEAM if, on top of the 22 factors and the current team, it helps in at least 3 of 4 years, the average gain is at least 0.010, and its similarity to every existing and selected factor is below 0.90. The team holds at most 6 factors.
A PAIR joins the team together if the two factors added together help in at least 3 of 4 years, the pair's average gain is at least 0.010, and the pair's gain is at least 0.010 more than the two factors' gains (each on top of the team) added up. Two factors that each got lucky simply add up their luck; real teamwork gives more than the sum.
A factor that is weak alone but shows some sign of life (helped in 2+ years, or helped one model) stays on the PROMISING list and can still join the team later in a pair. Nothing is rejected only for being weak alone.
For reference, factors made of pure random numbers rarely pass these bars; small gains are noise.

FORMULA LANGUAGE
Nested function calls only, no + - * / symbols. Windows n must be one of (1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 60).
- pct_change(x, n): x(t) / x(t-n) - 1
- lag(x, n): x(t-n)
- diff(x, n): x(t) - x(t-n)
- rolling_mean(x, n): mean of the last n values, ending at t
- rolling_std(x, n): sample std (ddof=1) of the last n values, ending at t (n >= 2)
- rolling_min(x, n): min of the last n values, ending at t
- rolling_max(x, n): max of the last n values, ending at t
- rolling_sum(x, n): sum of the last n values, ending at t
- zscore(x, n): (x(t) - rolling_mean(x, n)) / rolling_std(x, n) (n >= 2)
- add(a, b): a + b
- subtract(a, b): a - b
- multiply(a, b): a * b
- safe_divide(a, b): a / b, missing when |b| < 1e-12
- abs(x): |x|
- sign(x): -1, 0 or +1
- log(x): natural log, missing when x <= 0
Example: safe_divide(rolling_std(pct_change(AAPL_Close, 1), 5), rolling_std(pct_change(QQQ_Close, 1), 20))
